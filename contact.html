About AutoLL

AutoLL is an unofficial client for Lightning Lane Multi Pass and virtual queues at Walt Disney World. It is a personal project, built for one family's trip and shared as free software.

It adds two things to what it is built on. Autopilot watches a set of attractions through the day, alerts you when availability changes, and performs only the actions you have individually armed — booking, moving or swapping, inside a daily cap. NextLL is a focused search for one attraction when you want the earliest practical Lightning Lane right now.

What this is built on

AutoLL is a modified version of BG1, created by Joel Bruick. BG1 is the original project and the source of nearly everything underneath AutoLL: the Lightning Lane, virtual queue, DAS and itinerary clients, the interface, and the login flow. AutoLL is merged onto jgeurts/bg1, which restored Lightning Lane booking at Walt Disney World.

If AutoLL has been useful to you, the person to thank is Joel. His project, and his own way to support it, are at joelface.github.io/bg1.
